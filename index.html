1. MANNA: Unity-Based Maritime Simulation for Celestial Navigation

Ongoing research · Cranfield University

Development of a realistic maritime simulation environment in the Unity game engine for autonomous ship navigation research. The platform models marine environmental conditions, vessel dynamics and onboard sensors, providing a configurable, realistic and repeatable environment for developing, testing and validating the celestial navigation algorithms I have developed.

  • Maritime environment: realistic ocean waves and vessel floating behaviour, configurable wind, wave height and direction, and dynamic day/night cycles.
  • Dynamic celestial sky: star constellations and the Sun’s elevation and position change with simulation time and the vessel’s location.
  • Vessel dynamics: adjustable vessel size, mass and physical properties to study motion under different sea conditions.
  • Onboard sensors: IMU, inclinometer, camera and speed sensor, plus ground-truth logging of latitude, longitude, orientation and motion.
  • Algorithm validation: day and night experiments using Sun and star observations to evaluate celestial navigation performance against ground truth.
Unity Celestial Navigation IMU Sensor Fusion Marine Robotics

2. Autonomous Mobile Robot for Aircraft Inspection

MSc Thesis · Cranfield University

Developed a ROS 2-based autonomous inspection robot for aircraft wings and stabilisers using the Husarion Panther UGV with a custom telescoping camera column. Integrated 3D LiDAR, 2D LiDAR and stereo vision for mapping and navigation in GPS-denied hangars. Implemented hybrid SLAM (RTAB-Map & SLAM Toolbox) and Nav2 for autonomous path planning and obstacle avoidance, validated in Gazebo with accurate localisation, safe vertical inspection control and efficient defect imaging.

ROS 2 SLAM LiDAR Nav2 Gazebo

3. Generalised Anomaly Detection System

Published at IEEE ICAC 2025

Integrated real-time anomaly detection into a mobile robot to enhance public safety in dynamic environments such as shopping centres. Targets three anomalies: people who have fallen, abandoned bags and visible knives. The final pipeline combines YOLO-World v2 for open-vocabulary detection, YOLO-Pose for posture estimation and GPT-4V for contextual reasoning with JSON-formatted alerts.

YOLO-World YOLO-Pose GPT-4V ROS

4. Vision-Based Object Measurement System

C++ and OpenCV system that detects and measures objects in real time with a standard camera, using a £1 coin (23.43 mm) as a reference for automatic calibration. Converts pixel measurements into real-world units, removing the need for calipers or contact sensors, and is robust to varying lighting and object orientation for industrial quality control.

C++ OpenCV Computer Vision
